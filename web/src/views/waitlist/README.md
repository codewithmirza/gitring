# views/waitlist

The pages for people that the waitlist gate keeps out. `../../app/App.tsx` shows `Waitlist` instead of the app for them.

- `Waitlist.tsx`: the whole app of a person that the gate keeps out (landing, sign-in, interest).
- `Landing.tsx`: what gitring is, the product video, the scroll story (`../story/`), the closing join button, and the sign-in step.
- `Interest.tsx`: the interest page: pick a pack and say "Count me in".
- `copy.ts`: the words of these pages in one place, so a test can check them.
- `video.ts`: the launch video and its placeholder.
- `waitlist.css`: the styles of these pages.
